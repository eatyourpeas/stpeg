Join us for the STPEG Spring Meeting 2026 at Guy's Hospital, London. 

## About This Event

This meeting will bring together paediatric trainees, SAS, and paediatric consultants with special interest in endocrinology/diabetes from across the South Thames region.

Programme details, speaker information, and registration will be available in early 2026.

## Target Audience

- Paediatric trainees
- Specialty doctors (SAS)
- Paediatric consultants with special interest in endocrinology/diabetes
- Healthcare professionals in paediatric endocrinology
